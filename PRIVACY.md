# Privacy Policy – VVS-skiss 3D

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

VVS-skiss 3D is a free desktop app for sketching plumbing and heating installations in 3D. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## Summary

**VVS-skiss 3D does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your projects and files

- Your sketch is saved automatically on your own computer (in the app's local storage) so you can continue where you left off.
- Project files (`.json`) and PDF drawings are saved only where you choose in the Save dialog.
- Nothing you draw or type is sent to the developer or to any other party.

## Internet connections

The app works fully offline. When an internet connection is available, it loads its typefaces from **Google Fonts** (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). As with any web request, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information when the fonts are downloaded. No project content is included in these requests. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how Google handles this information. Without an internet connection, the app falls back to built-in system fonts.

The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to display its user interface. WebView2 is part of Windows and is covered by [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub page: <https://github.com/antonleigue/VVS-skiss-3D/issues>

### Svenska (kort)

VVS-skiss 3D samlar inte in, sparar eller delar några personuppgifter. Skisser och projektfiler sparas bara på din egen dator. När internet finns hämtas typsnitt från Google Fonts, och då ser Google din IP-adress. Inget projektinnehåll skickas.
